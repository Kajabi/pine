# Datepicker

Datepicker captures a single date, or in range mode a start and an end date. For a single date, people can type it as `YYYY-MM-DD` or pick it from a calendar that opens from the button in the field. The value is always a `YYYY-MM-DD` string (`YYYY-MM-DD/YYYY-MM-DD` for a range), so it never shifts by a day with the viewer's time zone.

## Guidelines

### When to use

- To capture one calendar date, such as a due date, a start date or a date in a filter.
- When `min` and `max` bound the dates people can choose.

### When not to use

- For a time of day, or a date and time together.
- For a date far in the past that people know by heart, such as a birth date. Typing is faster than paging back through the calendar.

### Accessibility

- Always set `label`. Use `hide-label` to hide it visually; it stays available to assistive technology.
- The calendar is a modal dialog with a date grid, following the [WAI-ARIA date picker dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/examples/datepicker-dialog/).
- The button that opens the calendar is named by `trigger-label`, followed by the selected date. Set `trigger-label`, `previous-month-label` and `next-month-label` when the page isn't in English.

### Format

`format` sets how the field shows dates and reads typed ones, with the tokens `YYYY`, `MM` or `M` (month), `DD` or `D` (day), and for times `HH` or `H` (24-hour), `hh` or `h` (12-hour), `mm` and `A` (AM/PM). Typed dates may drop leading zeros, and a typed ISO date is always accepted too.

The **value stays ISO** (`YYYY-MM-DD`, or `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`) whatever the format, so what a form submits and what the change event carries never depends on how the field reads. Backends parse ISO 8601 directly, as Rails' `Date.parse` and `Time.zone.parse` do.

### Range

Set `range` to pick a start and an end from two months side by side. The field looks and works like a single date's: type the range as `YYYY-MM-DD – YYYY-MM-DD` (a hyphen, `to` or `/` between the dates also works), or click the field to open the calendar beside it. While it's open, typing moves the calendar to the typed range.

In the calendar, the first pick starts the range and the second ends it; until then, the range follows the pointer or the keyboard, and the footer shows what Apply will set. Nothing changes until Apply; Cancel or `Escape` keeps the range as it was. Picking only a start applies a one-day range.

### Range with presets

`presets` lists ranges beside the calendar, followed by **Custom range**, which is marked when the range matches none of them and moves focus to the calendar. The change event carries the matching preset's `key`, so a filter can keep "Last 30 days" relative to today rather than store fixed dates. Set `custom-range-label` to translate it.

Pass built-in keys as a comma-separated list: `today`, `yesterday`, `last-7-days`, `last-30-days`, `last-90-days`, `this-month`, `last-month`, `this-quarter`, `last-quarter`, `this-year`, `last-year`, `year-to-date` and `last-12-months`. "Last 7 days" is the 7 days ending today; "This month", "This quarter" and "This year" are the whole period, and "Year to date" ends today. To translate the labels or add ranges of your own, set the property to an array of `{ key, label?, start?, end? }`. A preset that falls outside `min` and `max` is disabled.

### Range with times

`time-picker` adds hour and minute pickers below each month, with AM and PM on a 12-hour clock where the locale uses one. `minute-increment` sets the minutes' step. The value carries the times, as `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm/YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`. Unless `format` includes a time, the field adds one on the same clock as the pickers, as `YYYY-MM-DD 2:00 PM – YYYY-MM-DD 10:00 PM` in `en-US`. Without `range`, `time-picker` adds one time, and the date waits for Apply.

## Events

### pdsDatepickerChange

Emitted with `{ value }` when someone picks a day, or types a date and leaves the field or presses `Enter`. `value` is `YYYY-MM-DD`, or an empty string when the field is cleared. It doesn't emit when the `value` property is set programmatically, or when the typed text isn't a date that can be picked.

In range mode it emits on Apply, or on a typed range, with `{ value, start, end, preset }`: `value` is `YYYY-MM-DD/YYYY-MM-DD`, `start` and `end` are its two dates, and `preset` is the key of the preset the range matches, when it matches one. With the time picker, each date carries its time, as `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`, and the change waits for Apply.

### pdsFocus and pdsBlur

Emitted when focus moves into the datepicker from outside, and when it leaves. Moving between the field and the calendar doesn't emit them.

The inner field's own `pdsInput`, `pdsChange`, `pdsFocus`, `pdsBlur` and `pdsKeyDown` events stay inside the datepicker.

## Inside modals, popovers and other overlays

The datepicker works inside `pds-modal`, `pds-popover`, `pds-filter` and `pds-drawer`. `Escape` closes only the calendar, never the overlay around it, and picking a day doesn't dismiss the overlay. The calendar sizes itself to the window, scrolling with its footer pinned when there isn't room, and hides when its field scrolls out of view. On narrow screens a range shows one month at a time.

## Values, time zones and servers

- Values are plain ISO strings with no time zone: `YYYY-MM-DD`, and with the time picker `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`, a wall-clock time. Attach the zone on the server if the time needs one, as Rails' `Time.zone.parse` does with the account's zone.
- A range submits as one field, `start/end`. Split it on the server, as `start, finish = params[:period].split('/')`; each half parses with `Date.parse` or `Time.zone.parse`.

## Form Integration

- Set `name` and the date is submitted with its form as `YYYY-MM-DD`, or `YYYY-MM-DD/YYYY-MM-DD` in range mode.
- Resetting the form clears the date.
- With `required` and no date, typed text that isn't a date, or a date outside `min` and `max` (typed or set in code), the form won't submit and the field shows as invalid. The form reports `badInput` for text that isn't a date and `rangeUnderflow` or `rangeOverflow` for dates outside the range, with the messages set by `invalid-date-message`, `min-date-message` and `max-date-message`; `{date}` in the last two is replaced by the bound.
